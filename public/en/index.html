02 / TREATMENTS

IVF and ICSI: what is included?

01

Conventional IVF

Ovarian stimulation, egg retrieval, fertilization in the laboratory, embryo culture and possible transfer. Ask whether anesthesia, medication and freezing are included.

02

ICSI

A laboratory specialist injects a single sperm into an egg. It may be recommended for selected male-factor infertility or previous fertilization difficulties.

03

Care coordination

Prepare translated records, questions for your doctor, appointment dates, estimated travel time and a written breakdown of costs.

04 / PROCESS

IVF treatment process

01

Initial review

Collect hormone tests, ultrasound reports, semen analysis and prior treatment records.

02

Ovarian stimulation

Typically around 8–14 days, with ultrasound and blood monitoring as prescribed.

03

Egg retrieval

Often scheduled around 34–36 hours after the trigger injection.

04

Embryo laboratory

Fertilization and embryo development are assessed, often on day 3 or day 5–6.

05

Transfer and follow-up

Fresh or frozen transfer depends on the clinical plan; pregnancy testing follows the clinic’s schedule.

06 / MATERNITY

Postpartum care and recovery

IVF treatment and giving birth in Saipan are separate decisions. Families planning delivery on the island should independently confirm maternity hospital availability, newborn examinations, postpartum follow-up, accommodation, caregiver credentials and documentation requirements.

07 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is ICSI more successful than IVF?

Not necessarily. ICSI is useful for certain indications but does not guarantee a higher live-birth rate for every patient.

How many visits will I need?

Egg retrieval and embryo transfer may be arranged in one trip or separate trips. The usual 8–14 days of stimulation monitoring is not the total stay. Ask for a personal schedule with arrival, procedure and expected return dates before booking.

Are these Saipan clinic prices?

No. The amounts above are general overseas IVF planning ranges, not a confirmed Saipan clinic price list.

Can a child born in Saipan obtain US citizenship?

Citizenship and immigration matters require current official guidance and case-specific legal assessment; no outcome is guaranteed.
